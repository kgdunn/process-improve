- **The browser app (factori.al) suggests runs from a model of every run.** In Next, for a goal of the largest or
  smallest result and numeric factors, one press fits a Gaussian process of every run with a result and lists 1 to
  8 runs where it expects the most to gain (`suggest_next_runs`), each with why: it looks better than the best so
  far, or little is known there. Another press adds them to the run sheet as runs of the study.
