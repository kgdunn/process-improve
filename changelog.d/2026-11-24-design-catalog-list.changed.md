- **The design catalog's list (factori.al/catalog) has one short line for each design.** A line names a design by
  its family and size, marks what it estimates in the color of its cell in the table, and ends in a small "Open"
  button; a card a design held three or four designs to a screen. Entries that hold the same runs are one line:
  "printed in 72 places" opens onto them, each with its source and its own way to open, where the list repeated
  the 2^3 factorial 73 times. The count says how many are different: "1,404 designs, 750 different".
- **A design's picture in the list has a run a row and a factor a column, and a key over the list says so**, with
  its three colors for a low, a middle and a high setting. The picture lay across the card with nothing to say what
  it showed.
