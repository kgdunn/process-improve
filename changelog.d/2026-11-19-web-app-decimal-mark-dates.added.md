- **Factorial writes numbers with a decimal comma when a study's settings ask for it, and dates as the browser
  writes them.** "Settings" has a drop-down for the decimal mark: a period, the default for every new reader, or a
  comma. With a comma the page writes 1,5 in its sentences, tables, equations and charts, and reads 1,5 where a
  range is typed; the study keeps the choice. The Python code keeps the period. The date at the foot of the page
  and in a report's stamp is written as the reader's browser writes dates, the report's with its time zone.
