- **factori.al makes four more designs: OMARS, A-optimal, and Sobol and MaxPro space-filling designs.**
  An OMARS design has the three levels and clear main effects of a definitive screening design in
  the number of runs you choose above its own, for 3 to 5 factors. An A-optimal design is made for
  a model and inside limits on the settings, as the D- and I-optimal designs are. A Sobol sequence
  and a MaxPro design fill the ranges for no model, as the Latin hypercube does. All four are at the
  Adventurous reading level, and are chosen from the design list: the comparison of designs for a
  model does not search for them on every change of the brief. (#1223)
