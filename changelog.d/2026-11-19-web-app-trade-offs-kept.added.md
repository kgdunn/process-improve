- **Browser app (factori.al): a study keeps what its trade-off chart shows.** The responses on its axes, what colors
  its points, the limits typed, the level of each categorical factor and the settings chosen are kept with the study
  (study format 14), so a study opened again, from this browser, a link or a file, shows the chart as it was left.
  The settings tried are drawn again with the same seed, so the choice is found again among them.
