- factori.al can share a study in a locker, for two or more people to work on together: "Share this study", in Run, asks first, in a few plain lines that say who will see the study, what the server keeps and how to stop, then gives a link; the results typed in any browser in the locker reach the others. The study is encrypted in the browser with a key that is part of the link, after its `#`, so the server keeps it and cannot read it. How Factorial works explains lockers under "Can I work on a study with others?", with a picture of the study going through the server encrypted and the key going around it in the link, a line encrypted in front of the reader by the app's own code, and links to that code and the server's for anyone who wants to audit them. A result corrected in one browser reaches the others; one changed in two at once keeps the one that reached the locker first, and the page says which it replaced. Stop using the locker, or remove it for everyone: each browser keeps its own copy. The server's encrypted study locker, `deploy/factorial/encrypted_study_locker.py`, keeps one padded blob for each locker and nothing else, removes a locker nobody changes for 30 days, and logs nothing that names one.
