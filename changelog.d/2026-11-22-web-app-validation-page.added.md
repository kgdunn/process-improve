- **The browser app (factori.al) has a page on how it is validated.** `validation.html` says, on one screen,
  what Factorial's designs are checked against: the 22 books, handbooks and papers that print them, and the 9
  open-source packages in R and Python that build them, with the differences found and a grid of which kind of
  design was compared with which package. The footer's "Validation & technical accuracy" now opens it, and the
  textbooks' answers keep a link of their own, "Reproduced from the books". In the design catalog, a design the
  library makes itself has a "Checked" note: the sources that print a design of its kind and size, and the other
  packages that build it as the library does.
