- **Browser app (factori.al): the balance, a target and a specification of a mixture of four components or more
  are drawn over a triangle.** Each chart is over three components, at first the three with the largest shares of
  the blend found, the others held at that blend: the balanced blend, the blend that gives the targets, or the
  surest blend. One picker under each chart sets the three for all of them. The captions name what is held; the
  runs are not drawn on such a triangle.
