- **The browser app (factori.al) says how long a design's search takes on the reader's own device** (#1298). The
  wait said before an optimal design is made, and the 5 seconds from which a search is the reader's to start, came
  from one fast machine. The comparison of designs now times each search expected to take half a second or more;
  on a device that took longer than those times, a phone or an older laptop, every later wait is scaled by how much
  longer, for the session, and says "on this device". A device as fast or faster keeps the times as they were,
  since they are the most a search took. A search that was quick on the fast machine can then be the reader's to
  start on a slow one, with its wait said.
