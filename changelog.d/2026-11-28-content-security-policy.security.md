- factori.al serves every page with a Content-Security-Policy that names the only hosts a page may load from or send to (the site, jsDelivr and PyPI), and the app loads Plotly.js from jsDelivr only if its bytes are those of the Python plotly package's copy, checked by their SHA-384 (`plotly_js_integrity` in the build's manifest). The browser tests serve the app under the same policy.
