- **The design catalog opens its split-plot designs with their whole plots** (#1225). Eleven designs, among them
  Montgomery's tensile strength of paper, his plasma etch and oxide furnace, and Goos and Jones's wind tunnel, open
  in the browser app (factori.al) with each run's whole plot and block as the source prints them, and Learn tests
  each term against the spread it meets. An entry of the reference catalogue names its whole plots with
  `design["whole_plots"]`, a column or a list of columns, as it names its blocks; a column called whole plot is
  taken without it. A design was taken as split-plot wherever its text said "split", so Montgomery's two halves of a
  2^3 and his blocked central composite design (Problem 11.18) did not open; they now do.
