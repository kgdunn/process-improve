- **The browser app's (factori.al) figures** (#1242). "Average result at each setting" draws the model's interval
  for the mean at each setting, with the other factors averaged over the runs. A chart of four lines or fewer names
  each line where it ends, beside the legend. A figure's Python code shades the settings a study's limits rule out,
  as the page does, and numbers each residual by its run's place in the run order. The lines between a surface's
  bands take the page's colour, so they show on its dark bands. A figure in three dimensions takes a row of its own
  under "More charts", and is taller there. In a report, a surface with equal axes or a figure in three dimensions
  keeps a shape that suits it, rather than 720 by 340 pixels. A figure's window can save it on a dark background.
