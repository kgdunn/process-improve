- **The browser app's (factori.al) figures** (#1242). A figure's Python code shades the settings a study's limits
  rule out, as the page does, and numbers each residual by its run's place in the run order. The lines between a
  surface's bands take the page's colour, so they show on its dark bands. A figure in three dimensions takes a row
  of its own under "More charts". In a report, a surface with equal axes or a figure in three dimensions keeps a
  shape that suits it, rather than 720 by 340 pixels. A figure's window can save it on a dark background.
