- factori.al's What if is one table: a small picture of what each factor does to each response, a What-if row of boxes, sliders and level buttons under the factors, and each response's prediction drawn against its goal with a word (inside, likely inside, probably outside, outside), read off its 95% prediction interval, with the 20 runs you might get on the same drawing. Two lines lead it: the best run so far, and how many goals the What-if row's settings meet. Buttons set the row to the best for every goal, the best run, or the middle of the ranges; the table of intervals and leverage is a press away (#1503).
