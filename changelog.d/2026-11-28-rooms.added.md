- factori.al can share a study in a room, for two or more people to work on together: "Work on it together", in Run, gives a link, and the results typed in any browser in the room reach the others. The study is sealed in the browser with a key that only the link holds, after its `#`, so the server keeps it and cannot read it. A result corrected in one browser reaches the others; one changed in two at once keeps the one that reached the room first, and the page says which it replaced. Leave the room, or remove it for everyone: each browser keeps its own copy. The server's relay, `deploy/factorial/room_relay.py`, keeps one padded blob for each room and nothing else, removes a room nobody changes for 30 days, and logs nothing that names one.
