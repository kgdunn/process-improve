- factori.al can share a study in a room, for two or more people to work on together: "Work on it together", in Run, asks first, in a few plain lines that say who will see the study, what the server keeps and how to stop, then gives a link; the results typed in any browser in the room reach the others. The study is encrypted in the browser with a key that is part of the link, after its `#`, so the server keeps it and cannot read it. How Factorial works explains rooms under "Can I work on a study with others?", with a picture of the study going through the server encrypted and the key going around it in the link, a line encrypted in front of the reader by the app's own code, and links to that code and the server's for anyone who wants to audit them. A result corrected in one browser reaches the others; one changed in two at once keeps the one that reached the room first, and the page says which it replaced. Leave the room, or remove it for everyone: each browser keeps its own copy. The server's relay, `deploy/factorial/room_relay.py`, keeps one padded blob for each room and nothing else, removes a room nobody changes for 30 days, and logs nothing that names one.
