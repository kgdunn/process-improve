- Concept map: the names around a selected term no longer lie on each other where there is room for them. Each
  neighbour's name is drawn whatever it lands on; it now takes the side of its term (below, above, right or left)
  where it lands on least, by its size as drawn. Across six terms tried, with and without the book comparison,
  the pairs of names on each other fell from 28 to 5; those left are in clusters with no side clear. While books
  are compared, a topic's name too wide for its topic at the furthest zoom goes beside the topic, on the first side
  clear of the other topics and of the map's edge, so it no longer covers the sides of the topic's ring
  ([#1258](https://github.com/kgdunn/process-improve/issues/1258)).
