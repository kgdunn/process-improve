- **Factorial has settings that a study keeps, and the first is the colours of its charts.** "Settings", in the
  "Studies" menu, chooses among four Scientific colour maps (Crameri, 2018): batlow, devon, la jolla and grey. Each
  is even to the eye and reads in grey and without telling red from green, and a switch flips the chosen one. A study keeps its settings, in study
  format 12, so its link and its file show others what you see; a study's own settings always win, and a tick keeps
  them for your new studies. From format 12 on, a page keeps a setting it does not know and takes no notice of it.
