- **The browser app (factori.al) analyses a split-plot experiment run elsewhere.** In "Analyse data you have", a
  column can be marked as the whole plot (a batch, a day's oven load) and another as the block (a replicate). The
  factors that keep one setting inside every whole plot are taken as hard to change, and Learn tests each term
  against the spread it meets in a new table, a factor of several levels as one term, in place of the ANOVA table.
  Montgomery's tensile strength of paper (Section 14.4) gives the F tests and variance components of his Table 14.19.
  A study of a design the app makes with whole plots shows the same table, and its Python prints it.
