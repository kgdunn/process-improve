- **The browser app (factori.al) keeps a design option the reader sets while the designs are being compared**
  (#1355). Until the reader names a design, the page picks one when its comparison ends and puts that design's
  options in the form. A comparison that ended after the reader had set, say, 2 replicates put 1 back, and the run
  sheet was made with 1. An option the reader sets now counts as the reader's choice, as naming a design does.
