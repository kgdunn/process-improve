Why do numbers differ? Try:

The cases are those of the reference catalogue of process-improve, which holds each source's runs and every number it prints, with where it is printed. Its test suite recomputes each number on every change to the library, and fails unless the number agrees with the source to within the source's own rounding, or the catalogue records why it does not. A relative difference here is the library's value less the printed one, as a share of the printed one. Propose a case for the catalog.
