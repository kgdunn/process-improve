How we know the designs are right

Is Factorial accurate?

Factorial rebuilds the designs that 22 textbooks and papers print, and that 9 open-source packages build. It compares results, and where something is different, we state it clearly.

The books

22 books, handbooks and papers

Over 37,000 checks; nearly 35,000 match the books exactly.

  • Box, Hunter and Hunter, Statistics for Experimenters
  • Montgomery, Design and Analysis of Experiments
  • Box and Draper, Response Surfaces, Mixtures, and Ridge Analyses
  • The NIST/SEMATECH e-Handbook of Statistical Methods
  • Davies, The Design and Analysis of Industrial Experiments

And 17 more, each with its pages

See which design was checked against which

When we get different answers, we investigate each one:

  • 909 were misprints in the books. We show each one in the links above.
  • 9 differences found in other open-source packages. We have let each one know via their reporting channels.
  • 2 were gaps in Factorial. Both are on our public backlog list to fix.
