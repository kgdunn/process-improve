    A factor is numeric, from a low to a high value, or categorical, with two labels such as supplier A and B; the first label is coded -1 and the second +1. Choose each factor's type, or type two labels as its levels. Only the two-level designs take a categorical factor: full factorial, fractional factorial and Plackett-Burman.

    Factorial is the browser app of process-improve, the Python package that accompanies the book Process Improvement using Data.

    How this works: Pyodide runs CPython compiled to WebAssembly. The page loads it from a public CDN, installs process-improve into it, and runs the same generate_design, TrueProcess and analyze_experiment you would call in Python; the charts are the library's own plots, drawn with Plotly. Nothing is uploaded: your runs stay in this tab and in the workbook you download.
